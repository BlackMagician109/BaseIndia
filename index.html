About Us

Accurate, error-free detailing that puts clients at ease

Base India is headquartered in Gurgaon, the developing financial capital of India. We've carved out a niche in structural steel detailing and allied services, with over 500 projects to our name — started small in 2004 with a team of two engineers, grown on the trust of clients who came back project after project.

Within a short span we were handling structures from 300 to 2000 tons, on completion cycles of 3 to 12 weeks. Today we're a team capable of running 7–10 projects of varying size and complexity at once.

  • Accurate, error-free drawings — assessment, planning and general detailing
  • AISC & CISC standards, with added expertise in IS, BS and Australian codes
  • Current 2D drafting, 3D modelling and StaadPro workstations
  • Clients across India, the US and Canada
